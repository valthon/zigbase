### Internal
- Unmasked the `query/params.zig` (`parse`), `report/sentry.zig` (`buildEnvelope`), and `backend/postgres/conn.zig` (`parseDataRow`) tests by driving them on the raw, leak-detecting `std.testing.allocator`. Doing so surfaced three ownership-contract defects, all masked in production only because every real caller passes an arena (whose `free` is a no-op): `query/params.zig`'s `decode()` returned a percent-decoded sub-slice shorter than its scratch allocation — freeing it at that reported length is an invalid free under any non-arena allocator — and now dupes the decoded result into a right-sized buffer instead; `report/sentry.zig`'s `buildEnvelope()` left its intermediate JSON/header scratch un-freed after folding it into the returned body, and now frees each piece once it's no longer needed; `backend/postgres/conn.zig`'s `parseDataRow()` leaked the columns already parsed (plus the `vals` slice) when a later column in the same row was malformed, and now frees them on that error path. All three files are fully converted — their allowlist lines are removed.

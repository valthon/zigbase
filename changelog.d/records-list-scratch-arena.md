### Internal

- Made `records.list` self-freeing (allocator ownership contract 1): its entire query-compilation scratch — the joiner, the filter/rule parse+compile, the FTS/vector/ability/tenant/ttl predicates, the sort terms, the keyset decode/build, and the assembled page/count SQL — builds on a function-local arena and is freed, so `list` is leak-correct under any allocator instead of only when the caller passes a request/delivery arena. The escaping graph (the interned key set, the row items, and the minted next/prev cursors) stays on the caller allocator; cursors are duped out of the scratch arena, and `decodeCursor` runs on it too (subsuming the keyset codec's Parsed-discard scratch). This is a correctness/contract change, not a runtime memory change: every in-tree caller (the REST list handler, the `Data` facade) already passes an arena, so the scratch was reclaimed with that arena and still is — a scratch arena backed by an arena frees no capacity on deinit; the fix removes a latent leak for a non-arena (general-purpose) caller and lets the tests verify the path under the raw leak detector. On the back of it, 24 list-driving tests drop their arena masking (records.zig 62→42, policy.zig 15→12, data.zig 4→3 in the allocator-contract allowlist). The list tests still masked are gated on separate, still-unmigrated leaks — a `json`/multi-value field's `readValue` sub-tree, and the FTS5 index/trigger provisioning in `fts.ensureIndex` — noted in their allowlist justifications.

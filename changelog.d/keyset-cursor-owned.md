### Internal

- Made the keyset cursor-token codec (`query/keyset.zig`) allocator-contract correct. `parsePayload` decoded the wire payload with `std.json`'s default `.alloc_if_needed` and dropped the `Parsed` wrapper on the floor, so the returned `Cursor`'s `sort_str` aliased the transient base64 buffer and its parse arena was never freed — a latent use-after-free and leak that only stayed harmless because every caller (`records.list`) hands the decoder a request-scoped arena that outlives the cursor. The `Cursor` now owns its parse result (`.allocate=.alloc_always` + a retained `std.json.Parsed` freed by a new `Cursor.deinit`, contract 2), and the encoders plus `b64Decode` free their scratch (the latter's missing `errdefer` also plugged a real leak on a malformed-base64 token). No runtime change on the arena-backed production path; the value is closing the latent UAF and letting all 17 `query/keyset.zig` tests (the SQL-builder and the cursor codec) run under the raw leak detector — the file's allocator-contract allowlist entry is removed (17→0).

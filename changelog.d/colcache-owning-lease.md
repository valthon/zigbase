### Internal

- Closed a collection-cache leak on the uncached lease path (`colcache.zig`): when no cache is installed (Postgres, or tests), `lease()` loaded the collection into the caller's allocator but `release()` was a no-op, leaking the graph. The `Lease` now loads an uncached collection into a private, lease-owned `ArenaAllocator` that `release()` reclaims; the cache-hit/miss borrow path is unchanged (it only drops a refcount, never frees the shared copy). A per-lease arena is used deliberately rather than `Collection.deinit` — `Allocator.free` poisons freed bytes (0xaa) in safety builds, which would corrupt request-arena-aliased `col.name` that hooks/events read to request end; an arena `deinit` frees via `rawFree` (no poison), a no-op rewind under a request arena and a real reclaim under a raw allocator. Un-masks all 6 `colcache.zig` tests under the raw leak detector; allowlist entry removed (6→0).

Examples

Three examples, one ladder

ZigBase ships three buildable example projects that climb from the simplest possible use to the full framework surface: a packaging proof, then a realistic app, then the advanced framework a library integrator configures in code. Each builds against the published zigbase module.

  1. 1 Packaging proof

    Blog

    A minimal app on ZigBase-as-a-library — slugify hook + a Zigapagos frontend served with --serve-static.

    Golf simulator booking

    Airbnb for golf simulators — hooks, a business route, a DB-touching cron, and a Zigapagos frontend via the comptime-hardcoded static dir.
